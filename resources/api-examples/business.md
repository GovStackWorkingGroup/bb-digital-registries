# Business Registry API example

Use this example to retrieve a permitted business registration, resolve one by a declared exact key, or browse and search bounded result pages. The contract publishes a `businesses` Collection and a public business view, with conditional reads and caller-selected fields where those features are declared.

The [business Registry OpenAPI](../../api/examples/business-registry.openapi.yaml) is an illustrative deployment binding of the read operations in the [canonical DRAFT Registry contract](../api-reference.md), not a normative extension or a ratified business Registry profile. Its fields, disclosure policy, identifiers, search choices, and operational limits do not define universal business Registry requirements.
